Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Kingdomsalt collects, uses and shares your personal information when you use our online store, and your choices. Contact us at kingdomsalt@gmail.com with any privacy question.

What we collect

  • Order details: Your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and what you bought. You give us these at checkout.
  • Payment details: You enter these on Stripe's secure checkout page. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us whether the payment worked.
  • Messages: The information in any email you send us.
  • Basic technical data: Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, records standard information such as your IP address and browser when you visit the site, to keep it running and secure.

Your shopping cart is stored on your own device (in your browser's local storage), not on our servers, until you check out. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To process and deliver your order and arrange a refund, replacement or repair,
  • To send you order confirmations, tracking details and replies to your questions,
  • To keep records of sales and meet tax and legal obligations, and prevent fraud and misuse of the site.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not send you marketing emails unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes your payment and sends receipts.
  • Our supplier and its delivery partners. To deliver your order we give our overseas supplier (currently AliExpress and the sellers and couriers on it) your name, delivery address and phone number. Couriers may use your phone number to contact you about delivery.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and stores our order records.
  • Professional advisers, and government or law enforcement agencies, where the law requires.

Sending information overseas

Some of these providers are based or operate overseas, including in China, the United States and other countries. By placing an order you understand that your name, address and phone number are sent to our overseas supplier so that your order can be delivered. We take reasonable steps to share only what is needed to fulfil your order. Overseas providers may not be bound by the Australian Privacy Principles.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to deal with your order and any claim, and for at least 5 years where tax law requires. We then delete or de-identify them.

Keeping it safe

We keep orders in secure storage with access limited to us. No online system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (unless we must keep it by law). Email kingdomsalt@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the latest version on this page.
