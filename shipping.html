Shipping policy

Last updated: November 2026

At a glance

  • We deliver to Australian addresses only.
  • Shipping is free on every order. The price you see is the total price you pay, in Australian dollars (AUD).
  • Estimated delivery: 10 to 25 business days from the day you place your order.
  • Your items are sent from overseas by our supplier, so they take longer than goods sent from within Australia.
  • We never charge duties, customs or any other fee on delivery.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver to street addresses in Australia only. Some couriers cannot deliver to PO boxes or parcel lockers, so please give us a street address where someone can receive the parcel. We cannot deliver outside Australia.

How your order is sent

We do not hold stock in our own warehouse. When you place an order, we order your items from our supplier, who packs them and sends them directly to you from overseas. This is why delivery takes longer than you may be used to.

  1. Order placed. You pay through Stripe and we email you a receipt.
  2. Order sent to our supplier. We aim to do this within 2 business days of your order.
  3. Dispatch. The supplier packs and posts your order, usually within 1 to 5 business days. We send you tracking details when they are available.
  4. Delivery. International transit and Australia Post or local courier delivery follow.

Delivery times

Our estimated delivery time is 10 to 25 business days from the date you place your order. Business days are Monday to Friday, not including public holidays.

This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Delivery can take longer during busy periods such as Christmas, public holidays, sales events and in remote areas. If your order has not arrived after 25 business days, please email us (see below) and we will investigate and, if it is lost or has not been delivered, replace it or refund you in full. You do not need to wait until the 25 days are up to ask us for an update.

If you order more than one item, they may be sent separately and arrive on different days. You will not be charged any extra for this.

Shipping cost, duties and fees

Shipping to Australia is free. The price shown on the product and cart pages is the full price you pay. We do not add any extra shipping, handling, customs, import duty or delivery fee, and you will not be asked to pay anything to the courier when your parcel arrives.

Tracking your order

We email you tracking details as soon as the supplier provides them. Tracking updates can be slow or may pause while the parcel is in transit between countries. This is normal. If you can't find your order, email us with your order number and we will check.

Your delivery address

Please check your delivery address carefully at checkout. We send the parcel to the address you give us, so we cannot be responsible for a parcel delivered to an address that was entered incorrectly. If you notice a mistake, email us as soon as possible and we will try to correct it before the order is sent. Once an order has been sent we may not be able to change it.

Damaged, lost or wrong items

If your parcel arrives damaged, if an item is missing or wrong, or if the parcel is lost, you are entitled to a remedy. Email us with your order number and, where relevant, photos, and we will replace the item or refund you. See our refund policy for details.

Risk and ownership

The items remain our responsibility until they are delivered to you. If your parcel is lost or damaged in transit, we will deal with it as described above.

Contact us about shipping

Email hello@kingdomlightcandles.com.au with your order number. We reply within 3 business days.
