Refund and returns policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes those rights. If anything in this policy is inconsistent with the Australian Consumer Law, the Australian Consumer Law applies.

When you can get a refund, replacement or repair

The Australian Consumer Law says the goods you buy must:

  • Be of acceptable quality (safe, durable, free from defects and fit for the purposes they are normally used for),
  • Match the description, photos and any sample or demonstration model we show you,
  • Be fit for any purpose you told us about before buying, and
  • Come with the features and uses we have claimed for them.

If an item does not meet these guarantees, you are entitled to a remedy.

Major failure. A failure is major if, for example, a reasonable buyer who knew about the problem would not have bought the item, the item is significantly different from the description, is unsafe, or cannot be fixed within a reasonable time. For a major failure you can choose either a refund or a replacement, and you can also claim compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage.

Minor problem. If the problem is not a major failure, we can choose to repair the item, replace it or refund you, within a reasonable time. If we do not fix the problem within a reasonable time, you can then choose a refund or replacement.

Examples include an item that arrives broken or does not work, a zip or fastening that fails soon after normal use, a missing part, the wrong item, or an item that is clearly not what the photos and description showed.

How to ask for a remedy

  1. Email us at hello@kingdomlightcandles.com.au with your order number, the item and a short description of the problem.
  2. Where it helps, include clear photos or a short video of the item and the problem. Because items are sent from overseas, we may ask for photos instead of asking you to send the item back.
  3. We will reply within 3 business days and tell you what happens next.

There is no set time limit. You can ask for a remedy at any time within a reasonable period after you buy, based on what a reasonable person would expect the item to last. We will not refuse a remedy simply because a set number of days have passed.

Returns and postage

If we ask you to return an item for a replacement or refund, we pay the reasonable cost of returning it, or we will reimburse you for it. You will not have to pay to return a faulty item. We will give you the return address and instructions before you send anything.

If we agree the problem is ours, you will not be charged for the replacement item or its delivery.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid to the card or wallet you used to pay, through Stripe, in Australian dollars. We process approved refunds within 5 business days. After that, your bank or card provider may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show the money in your account.

A refund for a faulty or misdescribed item includes what you paid for it, including delivery.

Changing your mind

The Australian Consumer Law does not require us to give a refund because you simply changed your mind or chose the wrong colour or style. Because our items are ordered from an overseas supplier for each customer, we do not offer refunds or exchanges for change of mind once an order has been sent to the supplier. This does not affect your rights described above.

Cancelling an order. You can cancel an order for a full refund if you email us before we have sent it to our supplier. We aim to do this within 2 business days of your order, so please contact us as soon as you can.

If we cannot supply your order

If an item is out of stock or no longer available from our supplier, we will tell you and refund you in full for that item, unless you agree to a replacement.

Delivery problems

If your order is lost, has not arrived after 25 business days, or arrives damaged, you are entitled to a replacement or full refund. See our shipping policy.

Contact us

Email hello@kingdomlightcandles.com.au. If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your problem, you can contact your state or territory consumer protection agency or the ACCC. Information is available at accc.gov.au.
